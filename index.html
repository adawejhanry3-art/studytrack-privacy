STUDYTRACK Privacy Policy Last updated: October 15, 2026 STUDYTRACK ("the app") is a student study app with flashcards, deadlines, AI study tools and study-buddy features. This policy explains what information the app handles. Information we collect Account information: your email address and display name when you sign up or sign in (Firebase Authentication). Study content you create: decks, flashcards, deadlines, review history, lesson notes, and study-buddy messages and profile details you choose to enter. Some of this is stored on your device, and some is stored in our cloud database (Cloud Firestore) so features such as study buddies and leaderboards can work. Content you send to AI features: when you use the Lesson Summarizer, Word Lookup, AI Flashcards or Study Plan, the text, photo, or PDF text you submit (and, for Study Plan, your deadline and deck names) is sent to a third-party AI provider, Groq, to generate a response. Please do not submit sensitive personal information to these features. Technical information: basic app-integrity and diagnostic data handled by Firebase services (such as Firebase App Check). How we use information To provide and improve the app's features, keep your account secure, sync your study data, and generate AI responses you request. We do not sell your personal information and we do not show ads. Sharing We share data only with service providers needed to run the app: Google Firebase (authentication, database, app integrity) and Groq (AI processing of content you submit). Information you choose to share with other users, such as your study-buddy profile, is visible to those users. Storage and retention Local study data stays on your device until you delete it or uninstall the app. Cloud data is kept while your account is active. Your choices and deletion You can edit or delete your study content in the app. To delete your account and associated cloud data, email us at the address below and we will remove it. Children The app is intended for students aged 13 and older and is not directed to children under 13. Changes We may update this policy and will change the "Last updated" date when we do.
